About Me

Developer, problem solver, and lifelong learner.
```

Hi, I'm Jay. I'm a Python developer and Computer Engineering student with a strong interest in backend development, web applications, and software engineering.

I enjoy building practical applications and understanding how things work behind the scenes. My main focus is Python, but I've also worked with technologies such as Flask, FastAPI, JavaScript, React, Node.js, MongoDB, and SQL. I'm particularly interested in building reliable APIs, working with databases, and turning ideas into useful software.

My path into technology has been a little different from the usual one. Before focusing on software development, I studied translation and worked as an English teacher. That experience taught me how to communicate clearly, solve problems from different perspectives, and explain complex ideas in a simple way — skills that I now bring into software development.

This blog is where I document what I'm learning, the projects I'm building, and the problems I encounter along the way. I believe that building projects and sharing what you learn is one of the best ways to become a better developer.

I'm currently focused on strengthening my foundations in software engineering and becoming a stronger backend developer. There is always something new to learn, and I'm enjoying the process.
```
